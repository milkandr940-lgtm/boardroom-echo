Boardroom Echo

Boardroom Echo is a YouTube channel that turns short moments from business and money podcasts into hand-drawn stick-figure cartoons. Every clip credits the original show and links the full episode.

Boardroom Echo Uploader

The channel owner uses a small private desktop tool, "Boardroom Echo Uploader", built on the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner to:

  • upload the channel's own finished videos with title, description, tags and a scheduled publish time;
  • set thumbnails and channel branding (description, keywords, banner);
  • read basic statistics (views, likes, comments) of the channel's own videos.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's data.
