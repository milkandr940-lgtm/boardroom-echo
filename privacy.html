Privacy Policy — Boardroom Echo Uploader

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Boardroom Echo Uploader ("the tool") is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Boardroom Echo YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • An OAuth 2.0 token for the channel owner's own Google account, granted through Google's consent screen (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, youtube.force-ssl).
  • The channel owner's own channel information, video metadata and public statistics.

How the data is used

Only to upload and manage the channel owner's own videos and branding, and to show the owner statistics of those videos. No data of other YouTube users is collected.

Storage and sharing

The token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared, sold or transferred to third parties. No analytics, cookies or tracking are used. Statistics are kept locally for no longer than 30 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.

Contact

Through the Boardroom Echo YouTube channel page.
